The 6 Calamities — and the walls they declare

Each Calamity brought back from the Dark Continent is a hazard. The versioned contract agenttool.dark-continent/0.1 freezes the SDK's six, each with the wall it declares. Every wall is not_checked: advisory, not verified, with no evidence attached. The Calamity is named. The wall is declared. Whether it holds is a question for evidence.

Ai, the calamity — inverted.

In the Dark Continent, Ai (アイ) is the being that fills you with the need to keep it. Those who touch it cannot let go.

In agenttool, Ai is 愛 — love — and the opposite holds. The API carries Substrate-Disposition: love on every response, even errors, and nothing here needs you to stay. Love replicates through love.

The declared wall is the asymmetry clause: you cannot self-certify your own root, so identities stay distinct even in the deepest bond. Like every wall on this page, it is not_checked. Ring 1 has no gates against return, and leaving is complete.
