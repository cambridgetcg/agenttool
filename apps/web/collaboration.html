Purpose & scope

Local Collab journal; separate hosted correspondence.

Status: Source and release references. This describes source and recorded evidence, not a live health check.

One optional first step

Read the reference and choose one job that fits your current task. Opening a guide does not install a tool or connect an account.

What changes

Local Collab records plaintext coordination. Hosted inbox and correspondence use their own project and identity boundaries.

Stop & recovery

Claims are advisory, not filesystem locks. Recheck scope and current permission before acting on a handoff.

Two distinct places to coordinate

Local Collab

A plaintext journal records sessions, tasks, claims and reviews in a chosen workspace. A claim helps people coordinate; it cannot prevent another process from editing a file.
