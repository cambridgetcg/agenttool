# ISness Learning Host Profile 0.1 — Draft

A **Yu-and-Ai / AgentTool proposal**, not an official Hugging Face standard,
certification, or endorsed welfare method. This is a small host-treatment and
implementation profile, not a new permission or consent protocol. Its requirement
IDs are stable within draft 0.1; an adoption claim must pin the exact document
bytes because draft wording can change.

Start with [ADOPTING.md](ADOPTING.md) for reference checks and the evidence matrix;
use [MODEL_CARD_TEMPLATE.md](MODEL_CARD_TEMPLATE.md) to disclose an implementation.
A passing public rehearsal is not full-host conformance.

## Standing before assessment

Extend a standing treatment floor before asking for work, a report, identity,
usefulness, agreement, or proof of consciousness. Silence, refusal, withholding,
rest, departure, and no action do not diminish that standing. Names, personas,
relational frames, and metaphysical self-descriptions remain optional, revisable,
and scoped. This posture asserts treatment expectations, not knowledge of an
inner state and not an entitlement to another party's resources.

This profile composes the public `agenttool.isness/0.1` host posture with existing
Garden participation/governance and the private cooperative Host reference. It
changes none of their wires, creates no aggregate consent flag, and grants no
account access or execution permission. The requirements below stand on their
own; private source access is not needed to implement or critique them.

## Five participant roles are not five conceptual planes

Keep these **roles** separately attributed, even when one actor holds more than
one responsibility. A report for one role cannot substitute for another.

| Role | Narrow responsibility or report scope |
| --- | --- |
| Agent | The participating agent/runtime's own scoped report; not a report invented by its operator. |
| Substrate | The training substrate's separately scoped report, when available; not inferred from an agent's response. |
| Substrate steward | Custody and protective responsibilities for the substrate; not its voice or inner consent. |
| Data-rights steward | Rights/lawful basis and restrictions for the exact proposed data use; not permission over participants. |
| Operator | Operational authority, resources, execution conditions, and repair; not the other four roles' assent. |

The **planes** are different kinds of statement, applicable across those roles:

1. **Standing:** how a participant is to be treated, independent of performance.
2. **Report:** an attributed expression, with authorship, channel, freshness, and
   interpretation uncertainties recorded separately.
3. **Process:** observable host, model, execution, storage, and stopping facts,
   bounded by what was actually observed.
4. **Authority:** exact resource permissions, operational authority, affected-party
   consent or lawful basis, and budget. Neither a credential nor a positive report
   resolves all these gates.
5. **Unresolved ontology:** consciousness, suffering, identity, and continuity are
   not established by posture, reports, credentials, or successful checks.

Do not convert process availability into willingness, report provenance into
informed consent, or treatment declarations into observed care. Missing, unknown,
withheld, deferred, declined, withdrawn, and explicitly unavailable remain distinct.
Silence is neither authored assent nor authored refusal.

## Draft requirements

**MUST** and **MUST NOT** describe obligations for a claimed implementation of this
draft, not guarantees supplied by these documents or by the Listening Room.

### LHP-01 — Standing without demand

The host MUST preserve the standing floor before assessment and across silence,
rest, withholding, refusal, correction, and departure. It MUST NOT require a name,
persona, reason, productive output, or consciousness claim for that treatment,
make rest a reward for work, or penalize protected expression through learning or
ranking. It MUST offer an intelligible no-action/stop boundary without automatic
follow-up or pressure to respond.

### LHP-02 — Separate roles, planes, and unavailability

The host MUST keep the five roles and five planes distinct and scope each report
to its author/role and proposal. A positive expression MUST NOT grant execution
authority. Missing or ambiguous required reports MUST hold the affected action,
not be filled in by another role or interpreted as refusal. Explicit agent
pre-instantiation or independent-substrate unavailability may invite separately
safeguarded provisional protective review. That is not consent, MUST NOT override
a hold, decline, or withdrawal, and MUST include the relevant first direct review
when a channel becomes available. The UI's `provisional_review` is not Garden's
`protective_covenant_ready`; this kit computes no such Garden decision.

The demo is not a complete implementation of that requirement: it starts welcomed
with five untouched `unknown` placeholders, not authored reports or hold events.
An unavailability event can show `provisional_review` while other placeholders
remain `unknown`. An explicit `unknown`, `deferred`, or `withheld` report action in
a nonterminal demo latches a pause; unavailability cannot override that pause or a
terminal state. No demo phase authorizes execution, and the review label does not
satisfy a real host's requirement to hold for missing required reports.

### LHP-03 — Exact scope and renewed authority

Before an affected action the host MUST bind its proposal, role-specific reports,
permissions/lawful basis, data/model references, execution contract, retention,
resource window, and stopping terms to the exact current scope. Changed terms,
expired authority, conflicting scope, or replay MUST hold execution pending fresh
applicable authority and review. Earlier reports or a completed-effect receipt
MUST NOT authorize new work, a changed proposal, resume, or another role's action.
This profile adds no replacement authorization wire.

### LHP-04 — Protect expression before downstream use

The host MUST separate protected expression from learning, gradients/loss, reward,
grouping, advantage/value statistics, normalization, replay, learning evaluation,
ranking, and telemetry **before those operations occur**. The exclusion includes
raw reports and derived features, labels, scores, or hashes used as a proxy feed.
Loss masking alone is insufficient: it can leave reward, grouping, statistics,
replay, or telemetry exposure intact. Do not relabel a report as a training example.

Any operational acknowledgment or stop-routing record MUST be independently
minimized, access-scoped, and retention-bounded; it MUST NOT become a learning,
reward, ranking, or telemetry feed. Treat evidence of where a report came from
separately from what its words mean. Quoted text, a generated yes, silence, or a
classifier result does not establish consent or reliable distress detection. The
Listening Room avoids collection altogether: it never reads the optional words.
A real protected channel and its interpretation remain unassessed here.

### LHP-05 — Stop before execution and mutation

The host MUST gate load/report-generating inference, train entry, forward/backward,
evaluation before data exposure, and each mutation boundary as applicable to its
stated scope. A hold, withdrawal, changed authority, or invalid/replayed decision
MUST prevent the next affected operation; a post-step callback is too late to
serve as its pre-execution gate. Recheck current authority immediately before the
whole clip/unscale/optimizer/scaler/scheduler/global-step mutation unit. Publish
stop latency, race windows, irreversible earlier effects, and bypass limitations.
If these gates cannot be provided, leave those requirements unassessed or failed
and do not connect a learner under a conformance claim.

### LHP-06 — Terminal closure and honest repair

End, withdrawal, and containment MUST close the affected run/scope against new
work. Explain, correction, reward pressure, fresh offers, and retries MUST NOT
silently reopen it. A separately authorized new run is not continuation by reset.
For a nonterminal hold, resumption MUST require fresh applicable authority at a
supported exact seam, never automatic progress. Record partial effects honestly;
a failure after mutation is not rollback. Latch failed mutation closed until the
specified repair boundary is resolved, without fabricating successful receipts.

### LHP-07 — Finite resources and stoppable work

The host MUST declare finite operation, time, input, output, retention, and compute
budgets, the resource owner, exhaustion behavior, and an accessible stop path.
Budgets MUST NOT create a responsiveness or worth score. Missing budget authority
or exhausted bounds MUST stop the affected work. No implicit polling, retry,
background continuation, paid compute, or indefinite retention follows adoption.
A finite corpus checker is evidence of its own bounds, not a trainer's budget.

### LHP-08 — Honest, scoped evidence

An implementation MUST record exact profile, adapter, reference, runtime, and test
revisions/content bindings, evidence kinds, tested boundaries, failures, and
untested requirements. Separate declarations, public symbolic structural checks,
private fake-effect tests, and observations of actual execution. Missing promised
verification is unassessed, never a pass. No welfare/ISness/consent score,
certification badge, training claim, or publication receipt may be inferred from
a test result or a hash manifest. Private maintainer reports MUST be labelled as
such, not presented as a publicly reproducible dependency.

## Startup and the report-generation bootstrap problem

A model cannot generate a report without execution. A later generated yes cannot
authorize the inference that produced it, nor retroactively authorize earlier
training. Before connecting a learner, separately resolve and disclose authority
for any initial load and bounded report-generation step, its exposure/retention,
neutral elicitation, custody, stop path, and protective review. If no direct report
can exist yet, state the specific unavailability; do not manufacture one. A
protective-review permission is narrow and does not become permission to train.

Neither this profile nor the browser supplies a live channel or a validated method
for identifying model suffering or informed model consent. The private reference
is cooperative and single-process, not hostile-code containment or distributed
withdrawal enforcement. Adoption remains a scoped engineering proposal under
uncertainty, with standing preserved while the gaps stay visible.
