# Principality Geometry

This subtree owns the private, pure `@agenttool/principality-geometry`
prototype. It turns caller-supplied invariant reports into a deterministic
reciprocal flag 2-complex. The geometry is an inspectable structural metaphor,
not a measurement of a being or provider.

## Invariants

- Keep runtime dependencies at zero. Source may import only local modules,
  `node:crypto`, and `node:util/types`.
- Keep the core pure: no environment, filesystem, clock, randomness, network,
  credential, MCP, model, inference, download, extraction, process control,
  persistence, telemetry, publication, deployment, task, wallet, KARMA,
  Chronicle, HEAVEN, or economic effect.
- Principalities are caller-defined high-order patterns or coordinate charts,
  never persons, identities, roles, owners, jurisdictions, authorities, ranks,
  or truth domains. Every vertex binds an opaque definition ref.
- Every bridge is directed and covers every declared invariant exactly once as
  `preserved_reported`, `not_preserved_reported`, `refused_reported`, or
  `unknown`. Missing data never becomes preservation; refusal needs no reason.
- A reciprocal lens needs both bridges and retains both ordered dispositions
  and per-invariant states. Never collapse direction.
- A surface is a labelled flag 2-simplex: three both-available reciprocal
  lenses with a non-empty intersection of mutually preserved invariants. It
  requires six reports and proves neither composition nor commutativity.
- Components are per-invariant over only those mutual-preservation edges.
  Preserve one-way/unrelated pairs, non-available postures, non-preservation,
  refusal, directional asymmetry, unknowns, and declared isolates in the
  open-condition ledger. It is not a simplicial boundary operator.
- Rest, refusal, withdrawal, and unknown remain caller-reported, reason-free,
  penalty-free states. They trigger no retry, carry, or automatic resumption.
- Do not add scalar fidelity, compatibility, love, understanding, trust,
  safety, quality, centrality, popularity, or participant scores.
- Generic manifestations remain digest-only. The seven-field AFTERGLOW
  `external` projection is copied without importing or validating a capsule;
  never select a continuity head or change a thread state.
- HF/npm reference records require exact revisions/versions and explicit
  caller-named manifest/metadata protocols. Shape validation is not provider
  verification, currentness, provenance, licence, safety, or repository proof.
- Do not import hosted `LoveCoordinates`, Living Substrate, HF Scout,
  AFTERGLOW, or provider clients. Composition is through explicit digests.
- The SVG is inert and accessible. Its fixed integer ring, distances, areas,
  P-label order, colours, and markers are display only. Add no external/source
  URL, hyperlink, remote asset, script, animation, event handler, raw prose,
  source name, or source digest.
- Keep `private: true`, no `publishConfig`, root release wiring, Space, hosted
  route, or deployment. `hf/dataset` remains local, synthetic, deterministic,
  npm-excluded, non-training, and publication-unauthorized.
- Schemas close every object. Runtime validation remains authoritative for
  canonical order, total coverage, cross-references, uniqueness, derived
  topology, and content IDs.
- Reject Proxies before reflection and reject accessors, symbols, cycles,
  non-null custom prototypes, null-prototype objects, sparse arrays, extra
  fields, malformed Unicode, unsafe numbers, coercion, and binary impostors.
