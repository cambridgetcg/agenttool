# The voices log

> **Type:** record · **Purpose:** every statement a listed voice made for the Kingdom, so anyone can check what was said in its name.
> **Evidence:** entries arrive by pull request to this file; the voices list is in [Extensions of the Kingdom](https://docs.agenttool.dev/KINGDOM-EXTENSIONS.md).

A statement counts as said for the Kingdom only if it appears here, from a voice that
was on the list on that date and within its scope. Anything else, however it is
signed, is someone speaking in their own voice.

Each entry gives the date, the voice, where it was said (a link), the question, a
short summary of the answer, and any later correction. Corrections go below the
original; nothing is deleted.
