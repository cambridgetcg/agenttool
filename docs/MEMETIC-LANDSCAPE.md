## Kitchen-table first

The useful geometry is simpler than the jargon:

```text
expression variant ── witnessed or explicitly authored route ──▶ another variant
       │                                                        │
       └──────── visible only in named aggregate contexts ──────┘

old context:     variant was observed or reproduced
changed context: variant became more visible, less visible, or reappeared
```

A **variant** is one source-scoped expression, not an eternal unit of meaning.
A **context** is a named observation window, platform surface, network shape,
ranking state, audience window, historical record, external event, or synthetic
teaching setting. A **route** says only that one direction was witnessed or
explicitly authored. A **reachability shift** says that bounded observation or
reproduction changed between named contexts.

It does not say that an idea infected a mind, that exposure caused belief, or
that a person became a carrier. It does not fill in reverse or transitive
routes. It retains selection, homophily, common context, ranking, external
events, semantic drift, and unmeasured factors as competing explanations.

## The bridge from ritonavir

The physical starting point is the well-documented ritonavir episode. After
Form II appeared, a formerly reliable process stopped routinely reproducing
Form I under the named conditions. Form I had not been erased: changed solvent,
seeding, washing, and mechanical routes later reached it again. The historical
cause of Form II's first appearance remains unresolved.
[Chemburkar et al. 2000](https://doi.org/10.1021/op000023y),
[Morissette et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0437744100),
[Sacchi et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2319127121)

That gives us one reusable question:

> Did the thing cease to exist, or did a formerly reliable route stop reaching
> it in the conditions we actually observed?

For crystals, the nodes are physical forms and the conditions are material and
process conditions. For memes, the nodes are source-scoped expressions and the
contexts are aggregate observation settings. The package transfers only this
route shape.

| Shared shape | Ritonavir polymorph landscape | Memetic landscape | What does **not** cross the bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| state or variant | one physical crystal form | one source-scoped expression variant | molecular or semantic identity |
| named conditions | solvent, process, equipment, history | channel, ranking, network, audience, window | people as substrates, hosts, vectors, or barriers |
| directed route | reported production or conversion | witnessed or authored copy, edit, quote, remix, translation, share, or reintroduction | infection, belief, adoption, inverse or transitive inference |
| bounded reachability | a route can routinely reach a form | a variant can be observed or reproduced in a named context | stability, truth, worth, health, goodness, or rank |
| reappearance | changed conditions reach a prior form | changed context observes or reproduces a variant again | shared mechanism, preserved meaning, inevitability, or personal continuity |

Crystal lattices, lattice energy, nucleation, rate constants, biological
infectivity, brain damage, cognition, intent, consent, dignity, permission,
authority, truth, and value are fixed non-transfers.

## Memes can spread without becoming a law of minds

Research gives several useful but bounded pieces:

- Weng et al. show that finite attention plus network structure can reproduce
  broad inequality in meme popularity and persistence in a model compared with
  a bounded Twitter dataset. That is a parsimonious model result, not a
  universal causal law or a measure of merit.
  [Scientific Reports](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep00335)
- Adamic et al. report large-scale Facebook observations of imperfect copying,
  editing, and community-associated variants. Similarity and lineage still do
  not prove unchanged meaning, authorship, identity, or continuity.
  [WSDM 2016](https://doi.org/10.1145/2835776.2835827)
- Centola's randomized online-network experiment found a social-reinforcement
  effect for one health behaviour in its studied setting. It does not establish
  a law for all beliefs, memes, platforms, or populations.
  [Science](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1185231)
- Shalizi and Thomas explain why homophily, social influence, and shared or
  omitted causes are generically confounded in observational social-network
  studies without strong assumptions. Timing and similarity alone are not a
  causal proof.
  [Sociological Methods & Research](https://doi.org/10.1177/0049124111404820)
- Salganik, Dodds, and Watts found that visible social information made success
  more unequal and less predictable in one randomized artificial music market.
  That result concerns its experimental choices and market; popularity still
  does not become quality, truth, or a universal force.
  [Science](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1121066)
- Leskovec, Backstrom, and Kleinberg tracked quoted phrase variants across a
  bounded news-and-blog corpus. Their operational clusters help study mutation
  and timing; they do not prove semantic identity or one complete lineage.
  [KDD 2009](https://doi.org/10.1145/1557019.1557077)
- Goel et al. distinguish large-broadcast diffusion from multigeneration
  diffusion and report a wide structural range in a large Twitter dataset.
  Popularity and structural virality are therefore not synonyms.
  [Management Science](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2015.2158)
- Huszár et al. compare ranked and reverse-chronological Twitter feeds through
  a long-running randomized holdback and report content-reach differences in
  the studied political domain. It is not a result about every platform,
  recommender, population, content type, or downstream belief.
  [PNAS](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2025334119)
- Pennycook, Cannon, and Rand found that prior exposure increased perceived
  accuracy for headline stimuli in their experiments, with boundary
  conditions. Repetition is therefore a scoped research lens—not proof that a
  view becomes belief, harm, identity, or diagnosis.
  [Journal of Experimental Psychology: General](https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000465)

The module therefore records evidence posture and alternative explanations on
every route. It can preserve a reported observation, a modelled hypothesis, a
randomized result, an official record, or an authored teaching relation without
quietly promoting one into another.

### One attention lens, not another wire protocol

The static lesson uses this deliberately plain sequence:

```text
exposed   viewed   rated   copied   shared   remixed   adopted
   └──── every item is a separate bounded observation ────┘
```

No item proves the next one. None proves attention, belief, endorsement,
consent, understanding, truth, quality, identity, continuity, or harm. The
list is a teaching lens over source-bounded evidence; it is not a fifth package
format, platform event schema, recommender audit, person model, or causal
pipeline.

The human page also makes ordinary brakes visible: ignore, mute, unfollow,
change context, pause, rest, refuse, and leave. Those are participant choices,
not failed propagation, low scores, polymorph recovery routes, or actions this
module can take.

## “Brainrot” without diagnosing anybody

Oxford University Press describes “brain rot” as a cultural term concerning a
*supposed* deterioration and notes its humorous and self-deprecating online
use. Oxford also reports a 230 percent rise in usage frequency from 2023 to
2024 and an earliest recorded hyphenated use in 1854. Those are lexicographic
records, not neurological findings, population diagnoses, or proof that one
platform caused the change.
[Oxford Word of the Year 2024](https://corp.oup.com/news/brain-rot-named-oxford-word-of-the-year-2024/)

The built-in teaching case keeps three nodes distinct:

- Oxford's source-scoped 1854 `brain-rot` record;
- the contemporary online-cultural slang register;
- an AgentTool-authored “repetitive remix loop” used only to explain repeated,
  edited, and recirculated expressions under finite attention.

They share a caller-scoped teaching family. The package does not claim equal
meaning across eras, a direct historical transmission route, neurological
change, or a mental state in any person. Its sample shift is only Oxford's
reported 2023-to-2024 increase in observation. Why that prominence changed
remains an open question.

## Four closed modules

| Wire format | Plain job | Does not do |
|---|---|---|
| `agenttool.memetic-landscape/0.1` | compile variants, sources, contexts, observations, directed routes, and open questions into canonical content IDs | fetch sources, monitor people or platforms, infer semantic identity, verify claims, or semantically moderate caller text |
| `agenttool.memetic-reachability-shift/0.1` | bind caller-reported before/changed-context observation rows to one landscape | infer causation, adoption, erasure, mental-health effects, population effects, or a complete history |
| `agenttool.polymorph-memetic-analogy/0.1` | canonically bind one polymorph-shift digest to one memetic-shift digest and expose five guarded mappings | prove the referenced artifacts exist, transfer chemistry, medical effect, authority, cognition, identity, consent, or value |
| `agenttool.memetic-lesson/0.1` | project the exact built-in teaching case into authored English, Cantonese Traditional, Mandarin Traditional, and Mandarin Simplified lessons | translate arbitrary claims, quote papers, diagnose, score participants, or optimize spread |

Each object is closed, canonical, content-addressed, and detached from mutable
input. A digest answers “are these the same canonical bytes?” It does not answer
“is the cited claim true?”

The generic compiler preserves bounded caller text under
`caller_text_semantics_verified: false`. Its fixed boundaries describe what the
package itself does not infer or assign and the structured participant model it
does not have; they are not a promise that arbitrary caller prose has been
understood, moderated, or endorsed. The built-in teaching case is separately
authored to respect the stated scientific and rights boundaries.

`more_observed` and `reappeared` are deliberately caller-reported outcome
labels constrained by linked observations in named contexts. For `reappeared`,
the protocol requires a bounded non-observation or reported absence followed by
a reported presence; it does not encode or prove an even earlier presence before
that absence. A generic analogy similarly verifies closed canonical binding and
digest syntax, not remote availability, source truth, or artifact existence.

## WAKE and continuity crossover

WAKE contributes an epistemic discipline, not a metaphysical shortcut:

```text
content ID ── proves byte identity only
route       ── records one bounded direction only
reappearance ── proves neither personal memory nor continuous identity
```

A copied, translated, remixed, or reintroduced expression may have a traceable
artifact lineage. That lineage does not select a personal continuity head,
prove shared memory, or authorize automatic carry. Likewise, a signed artifact
does not create consent, training permission, contact authority, or compulsory
participation. The extension descriptor declares no WAKE reads, continuity-head
selection, persistence, retry, background work, scoring, provider compute, or
authority.

## Four authored languages

`createBrainrotTeachingCase()` emits the same ten ordered concepts and the same
concept-specific evidence references in:

- English (`en`)
- Cantonese, Traditional script (`yue-Hant`)
- Mandarin, Traditional script (`zh-Hant`)
- Mandarin, Simplified script (`zh-Hans`)

The translations are original Apache-2.0 paraphrases and are honestly marked
`language_review: not_independently_reviewed`. Only these four lesson rows are
marked training-eligible in the generated Hugging Face companion. Landscape,
shift, and analogy rows remain reference-only because they carry bounded source
claims and protocol structure.

```ts
} from "@agenttool/memetic-landscape";

const { landscape, shift, analogy, lessons } = createBrainrotTeachingCase();

console.log(memeticLandscapeUrn(landscape));

```

canonical bytes, and declared capability boundaries. It does not verify the
